# Document evidence playbook

## Sequence

1. Use Document Search for relevant passages and metadata filters.
2. Use Document Reader for exact quotations, revisions, or side-by-side
   comparison of identified documents.
3. Use Document Aggregator for exhaustive counts or lists; keep its exact count
   separate from semantic candidates.
4. Cite document identity, revision, date, and the evidence actually read.

## Evidence boundary

Search results are ranked candidates, not an exhaustive inventory. A document
count is not a count of search hits, and a passage cannot establish a current
database value without a matching structured record.

## Forbidden shortcuts

- Do not quote an unread document or infer its contents from a title.
- Do not add semantic candidates to an exact aggregate count.
- Do not silently combine revisions or document types.
